1.1.1-alpha — first public Palished release

Repair-bench R / Ctrl+R shortcuts, localized button hints, and a compact equipped-tool durability HUD. Normal costs and the Repair All confirmation are preserved. Client only; requires UE4SS. Disable UE4SS Ctrl+R hot reload to avoid a shortcut conflict.

Already using PKRepairKeys? Replace its existing folder; do not install a second copy.

We’d love to hear how Palished feels in your game! Leave a comment, share your experience, or report a bug so we can investigate and improve it: https://github.com/PikouGame/Palished/issues
