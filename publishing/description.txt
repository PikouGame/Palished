Palished
Small details. Smoother everyday play.

Keep an eye on your equipped tool’s durability, repair with a keypress, and enjoy tidier equipment slots that fit Palworld’s existing interface.

The little improvements
• A compact durability bar and percentage for your equipped tool or weapon. Orange below 30%, red below 10%.
• R repairs the selected item at a repair bench; Ctrl+R opens the normal Repair All confirmation. The buttons show the shortcuts in your current language.
• Tidier equipment columns: aligned slots, consistent section spacing, and Sphere Module level with the last accessory row. Native styling, slot sizes and controls are preserved.
• Empty hands get the game's native fist icon and localized Unarmed label.

Getting started
Client only. Requires a recent UE4SS loader for Palworld; no server installation is needed.

1. Close Palworld.
2. Copy Palished into your existing UE4SS Mods folder.
3. UE4SS hot reload uses Ctrl+R by default. Set EnableHotReloadSystem = 0 in UE4SS-settings.ini, or change HotReloadKey.
4. Restart the game.

Updating from 1.1.2 or earlier? Delete the old mod folder and its mods-list line first; see INSTALL.txt.

A small note
Repair costs and confirmation stay vanilla. Tested on Palworld 1.0.5.102999 with UE4SS 2281fa31 through Proton/Wine. Other game versions and UI mods may need adjustments.
